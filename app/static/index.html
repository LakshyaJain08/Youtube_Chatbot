What YouTube Chatbot Can Do

Verified Grounding

Precision Timestamp Citations

Every response cites exact [MM:SS] timestamps. Click any citation to seek the player directly to the moment it was spoken.

Staged Hybrid RAG

Semantic & Lexical Retrieval

Combines Google Text Embeddings with FAISS dense vector search and BM25 keyword matching to eliminate hallucinations and retrieve accurate context.

AI Synthesis

Executive Summaries & Takeaways

Instantly distills multi-hour discussions, lectures, and tutorials into key bullet points, chapter breakdowns, and interactive review quizzes.

Synchronized Player

Split-Screen Video & Metadata

Watch the embedded 16:9 player side-by-side with your chat. View duration, speaker info, extracted chunks, and total word count in real time.

Live Filter

Real-Time Transcript Search

Browse through hundreds of timestamped segments. Filter sentences with instant keyword search and click any line to seek playback.

Web Augmentation

DuckDuckGo Web Search Agent

Need information beyond the video? Toggle live web search to pull fresh external facts, documentation, or background information seamlessly.

Switch between conversations effortlessly. All your video chats, metadata, and insights remain safely saved in local storage.
